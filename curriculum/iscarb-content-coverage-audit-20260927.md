## Cross-chapter findings

1. **No critical missing topic was found that justifies changing the pinned 20-slide scientific route.**
2. The classroom route consistently covers the **mechanism needed for engineering judgment**; the full source ledger remains the place for historical examples, secondary subtopics and deeper specialist detail.
3. The AI material remains a **transfer/practice layer**: each chapter applies the existing software-engineering concept to an AI-containing system without creating a new formal AI outcome.
4. The strongest recurrent presentation weakness was **source-figure legibility**, not content coverage. Raster source figures often contain large white margins or low effective resolution. The shared runtime now crops/resamples only the in-slide preview while preserving the original figure and source link unchanged.
5. A visual should be considered classroom-readable only when the informative region occupies a substantial fraction of the figure panel at 1440×900. Live browser QA now measures this condition for raster figures.

## Research-use boundary

This audit supports **implementation fidelity and curricular coverage** only. It does not show that students learned the concepts, that iSCARB is superior to another method, or that every detail in Sommerville is represented on the 20-slide route. Outcome claims remain subject to the planned end-of-term evaluation.
