iSCARB × NELC alignment

Five principles used as design requirements

1 · Learning first

Each chapter starts from five measurable learning objectives, the disciplinary source and the evidence students should be able to produce.

2 · Clear educational value

AI is added only where it expands practice, feedback, critique, transfer or simulation; it is not a separate formal learning outcome.

3 · Human-centered design

The learner performs the core reasoning. The instructor retains educational and professional judgment. AI supports rather than replaces either role.

4 · Responsible design

Uncertainty, source provenance, AI disclosure, bounded use and human review are visible in the learning and assessment workflow.

5 · Evidence-based improvement

Learning evidence, transfer, revision and learner experience matter; output quality or simple usage counts are not treated as proof of learning.

Six-stage NELC → iSCARB crosswalk

1Define · ماذا نريد أن يتعلم المتعلم؟

Five chapter objectives, named source concepts, target artifact and observable evidence.

2Design · كيف ستحدث عملية التعلم؟

Hook, concept map, one evolving story, source-grounded explanation, three short stations and reflection.

3Enhance · أين يضيف الذكاء الاصطناعي قيمة؟

AI lens and transfer/practice challenge are used only after the core mechanism is established and for a defined learning benefit.

4Distribute roles · من يؤدي كل دور، ولماذا؟

Student: reason and decide. AI: support practice/critique/simulation. Instructor: design, guide, verify and judge.

5Assess · كيف نتحقق من التعلم في وجود AI؟

Independent reasoning, commitment, changed evidence, REFIT, AI-use declaration, human review and the published rubric.

6Verify & improve · هل حقق التصميم غرضه؟

Readiness checks, source-use evidence, rubric review, feedback and course evaluation feed the next design iteration.

What students will see in every chapter

20-slide classroom sequence stays intact

The scientific chapter content, five objectives, three stations, source review and assessed Mastery-v2 assignment remain unchanged. Slide 20 is explicitly presented as Readiness & next step.

NELC alignment is always one click away

Every lecture displays a small NELC aligned · 2026 badge and a dedicated alignment panel showing the five principles, six stages and the learner/AI/instructor role boundary for that chapter.

Role boundary

RoleWhat remains its responsibility
LearnerAttempt, explain, justify, verify, revise and disclose AI assistance.
AISupport practice, critique, alternatives, simulation or feedback inside defined boundaries. It is not independent evidence or an automatic grader.
InstructorSet the learning outcome and criteria, validate content and context, review evidence and retain final educational judgment.

Scope statement

“Aligned with NELC” means design alignment with the published framework. It does not mean that NELC has certified, approved, endorsed or partnered with iSCARB or CPIT-455. Any future formal recognition would require a separate process with the Center.
