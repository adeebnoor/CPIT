01 · Jaheziah

What can the learner do independently?

Chapter concepts build knowledge. Applying them develops skills. Decision boundaries and accountability demonstrate values. Each chapter specifies evidence and a review criterion while retaining its existing objectives and assignments.

Knowledge

Explain an engineering concept from its source and distinguish it from a related concept.

Skills

Apply the concept to the case and produce an inspectable calculation, test or contract.

Values and responsibility

Disclose assistance and limits, name the responsible owner, and revise judgment when evidence changes.

This proposed educational mapping supports readiness. It does not assign official Jaheziah specialization codes or exam weights; those require the approved program crosswalk. Practice questions are authored for this course, not taken from the Jaheziah examination.

02 · NELC · September 2026 framework

Five principles translated into design decisions

01

Learning first

The five objectives and named readings come before the choice of AI assistance.

Evidence: chapter map + source review

02

Clear educational value

AI supports critique of an alternative or examination of an assumption tied to the chapter concept.

Evidence: chapter-specific AI challenge; assistance is optional unless assigned

03

Human-centered design

The learner produces the attempt and argument; the instructor verifies and retains judgment.

Evidence: independent attempt + explanation + human review

04

Responsible design

Use non-sensitive teaching inputs, disclose assistance and check claims independently.

Evidence: AI-use gate + source + claim boundaries

05

Evidence-based improvement

Review the quality of reasoning and revision, then adjust teaching when misconceptions appear.

Evidence: initial and revised decisions + rubric + feedback

Six stages and where to find their evidence

StageApplication in iSCARBEvidence location

A plan to evaluate and improve learning

What to reviewEvidenceImprovement decision
Independent understandingAn instructor-reviewed explanation or new case, with explicit conditions for AI use.Revisit the misunderstood concept and check understanding again.
Value of assistanceThe first attempt, the revision and the reason for accepting or rejecting it.Adjust the hint or reduce support if it starts to replace the learner’s effort.
Quality of reasoningFIT–BOUND–ACT–EVIDENCE followed by STRESS–REFIT, assessed using the rubric.Adjust the case or feedback to address the observed weakness.
Workload and accessibilityLearner and instructor feedback, including reading and navigation difficulties.Simplify presentation and support while preserving the learning outcome.

Student drafts are saved locally and can be exported. Official submission, deadlines and grades remain in Blackboard. Experience feedback informs improvement but is not sufficient evidence of learning.
