# CPIT-455 learning-path review

## Finding and response

The uploaded simplified HTML files reused a common classroom route. In that route, some original chapter topics were moved into the same optional area as additional iSCARB activities. This confused source scope with classroom timing. Several figure placements and chapter references also carried over from another chapter.

The revised files distinguish the classroom sequence, required original-source review, and the iSCARB toolkit. Classroom sequences contain 16–19 units and three planned interactions. Original source review remains required where the instructor assigns it or the classroom has not completed it. The route does not make an unfinished source topic optional.

The source text, tables and available embedded source pictures are accessible in each chapter. Original PDF/PPTX downloads retain full slide layout and vector diagrams. This is an audit against the supplied packs, not a line-by-line audit of the entire textbook.

Strengthened topics include reliability measurement and operational profiles; static analysis and safety cases; reuse planning and all fifteen reuse approaches in the supplied table; component interface semantics, OCL and composition trade-offs; SaaS and multi-tenancy; and SoS architecture patterns and frameworks. Misplaced diagrams in Chapters 16, 17 and 20 were reassociated with their proper topics. Reused Chapter 10 recap figures are identified as recaps.
