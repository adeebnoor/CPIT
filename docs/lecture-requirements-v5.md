## Runtime notes

- Slides that carry source lists, a transfer case or a second question are marked `dense`: the body grid keeps a
  minimum height and the slide scrolls inside the stage instead of hiding content. Source lists shrink by at most
  20% before the slide scrolls.
- The glossary wrapper skips the case strip, code, buttons and links. Definitions appear inline only in modals
  (stations, quiz); on slides they appear in the **NEW TERMS** strip and on hover.
