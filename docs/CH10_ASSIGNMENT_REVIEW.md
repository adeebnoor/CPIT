# Chapter 10 assignment review — reviewed v5

The instructor requested a suitability review before restoring the Chapter 10 assignment. Other ISCARB lectures and assignments remain withdrawn.

## Assessment validity

The previous assignment repeated the lecture's desalination case. Its later shared-pressure-transmitter fact already appears in the lecture's R11 worked example and model answer. The reviewed assignment instead uses a fictional university registration service and a later operational dependency, giving students a near-transfer task with familiar dependability principles.

The activity progresses from an ungraded warm-up and feedback to an independent first decision and a later revision. It requires no code, new engineering metrics, real infrastructure testing, or external data collection. Estimated workload is 30–40 minutes.

## Alignment to the approved lecture

These are the lecture's own learning outcomes, not a newly asserted official programme mapping.

| Lecture capability | Assessed evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Explain dependability as trust across relevant properties (lecture outcome 1) | FIT selects and explains the property for the stated users and service. |
| Build a bounded verdict with inspectable, owned evidence (lecture outcome 4) | BOUND states a reopening condition; ACT and EVIDENCE name an action, evidence and reviewer. |
| Defend the verdict when a constraint changes (lecture outcome 5) | REFIT compares the new facts with the original boundary and explains the final decision. |

The four rubric criteria remain FIT, BOUND, ACT + EVIDENCE, and REFIT, one point each, with explicit 1 / 0.5 / 0 descriptors. A defensible RETAIN, REVISE or REPLACE can receive full credit. Browser completeness checks do not grade reasoning. Official deadlines and contribution to the course grade remain governed by the LMS and course syllabus.

Warm-up feedback is available to the student. The assessed scenario does not include a model solution. Students must distinguish provided facts, assumptions, and evidence still needed, and cite a relevant lecture unit or source page without inventing measurements.

## Workflow and preservation

The established local Student ID access flow is retained. Current drafts use `fbr:cpit455:ch10:reviewed:v5`; previous `fbr:cpit455:ch10:prod:v4` drafts remain untouched and separately exportable. Old responses are never silently relabelled as answers to the new scenario.

Part A is confirmed and saved before the new evidence is requested. Students can save incomplete backups and continue Part B after reloading. Final exports include the scenario/version, frozen initial reasoning, new evidence, final reasoning, AI declaration and human review. Saving, committing and exporting do not submit to the LMS.

This is an open-book educational sequence, not a secure examination service. Client-side state and public files do not prove authenticity or conceal the new evidence against source inspection.
