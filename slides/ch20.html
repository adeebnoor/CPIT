2. Policy Triage

What to control vs. let emerge

  • Control: Security, data formats
  • Emerge: User workflows, optimizations

3. Leverage at Interfaces

Influence through connections

Focus effort on APIs and protocols, not internal system changes

4. Ensuring Cooperation

Incentives for participation

  • Financial rewards
  • Access to resources
  • Regulatory compliance
