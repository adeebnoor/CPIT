WHY ISCARB
From covering content to proving capability.
Traditional lecture
  • Explains concepts
  • Lists CLOs
  • Uses examples after explanation
  • Ends with recap or quiz
  • Often hides the evidence chain
  • AI output may be accepted at face value
ISCARB lecture
  • Starts with an ill-structured engineering decision
  • Forces prediction and first-principles reasoning
  • Requires alternatives and trade-offs
  • Tests claims through falsification and evidence
  • Connects assessment to readiness conservatively
  • Ends with bounded approval, redesign or rejection
