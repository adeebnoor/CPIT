Dense source-grounded reading version with provenance, readiness and the full technical trace.

Teaching moves, student actions, expected evidence, rubric and facilitation guidance.

Machine-readable audit record of units, source separation, ETEC alignment and release status.



WHY ISCARB

Traditional conversion
  • Summarizes content into bullets
  • One template repeated across slides
  • Examples without engineering consequence
  • CLOs stated but weakly evidenced
  • AI output trusted because it looks polished
ISCARB
  • Preserves the complete primary source
  • Uses different visual grammar for different reasoning tasks
  • Requires alternatives, trade-offs and falsification
  • Ends in evidence and bounded assurance
  • AI proposals are audited before release
