The badge is issued only when the full release path passes. A blocked or quota-interrupted artifact remains reviewable, but it is not presented as verified.

Cumulative Fidelity

WHY USE IT?

From coverage to capability.

PILOT PRINCIPLE

Discover → Try → Trust → Teach → Reuse

Do not ask a faculty member to “adopt a framework” on day one. Let them use a better lecture, see the evidence trail, teach it, and decide whether the workflow earns a place in their course.

Recommended first pilot: one lecture per faculty member, then three consecutive weeks only if they choose to continue.
