# ISCARB Master Guidelines v4.7.0

These are release conditions for every advanced software-engineering lecture, independent of topic.

1. **Cognitive load** — PRIMARY source narrative never becomes a slide paragraph. Presenter source content is limited to short engineering maxims; full conditions remain in P1 and speaker notes.
2. **Visual-text alignment** — P1 figures are preferred. Public images are allowed only when semantically aligned. The same asset may not be reused in another Unit unless Unit 17 explicitly mutates a constraint on that visual.
3. **Bounded local context** — Unit 11 uses an explicitly hypothetical Saudi/local constraint and only mechanisms already taught in the current P1 lecture.
4. **Scalability / trend** — Unit 13 must mutate a numeric or structural variable and ask which assumption fails first. Open-ended “what changes?” prompts do not pass.
5. **AI governance** — Unit 15 separates AI-assisted option/test generation from human professional sign-off. AI may not certify correctness, safety, security, or absence of defects.
6. **Performance grading** — Unit 19 and Unit 20 require a learner artifact and P1 evidence to defend every capability claim and final decision.

The six rules are enforced by Gate v16 and are therefore part of the release contract, not style suggestions.
