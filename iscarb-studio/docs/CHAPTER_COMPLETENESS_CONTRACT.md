# Chapter Completeness Contract

This release hardens ISCARB/CIMT lecture compilation around a no-drop primary-source contract.

## Release rules

- P1 remains the complete mandatory technical scope for the 90-minute lecture.
- Every major chapter checkpoint must be taught by Unit 15; Units 16–20 synthesize, critique, assess, and prove capability.
- Coverage is not satisfied by metadata, a title, or a takeaway alone: the source-supported technical substance must appear in learner-facing `core_content`.
- Administrative slides (assignment logistics, deadlines, marks, course furniture) do not consume major technical coverage slots.
- The presenter uses source-native visual jobs and CIMT classroom grammar rather than a unit-number template.
- The classroom choreography is learn-by-doing: frame/predict, derive, implement, measure, falsify, review, mutate constraints, critique, defend, prove, decide.
- Readiness is an evidence claim, not a badge. A quota-safe deterministic fallback remains BLOCKED / REVIEW REQUIRED and leaves standardized readiness unverified rather than inventing an SLO mapping.
- Unsupported quantitative thresholds and percentages are rejected.

## Regression scope

The chapter-completeness regression covers the archived CPIT-455 CIMT lecture set for Chapters 10–17 and verifies:

- numbered source sections are retained as major checkpoints;
- every major checkpoint is represented by Unit 15 in the quota-safe draft;
- the fixed lecture contract remains exactly 20 units and 90 minutes;
- prompt inheritance includes IDR-1..14, EER-1..12, and Unit 1..20 functions;
- all archived chapter examples render 20-slide HTML/PDF/PPTX presenter outputs;
- existing source-visual, export, source-first-title, numeric-precision, and sparse-blueprint regressions continue to pass.

This contract is intentionally conservative: it proves completeness relative to the supplied P1 material. It does not claim coverage of textbook pages or external material that was not supplied as P1/S#.
