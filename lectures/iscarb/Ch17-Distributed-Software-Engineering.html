CRISIS · Start with the case01 / 20 · 2 min

Distributed Software Engineering

Constructed teaching scenario · Chapter 17 · 2 minutes

A national online-exam service must receive submissions from several regions during a predictable peak. It needs bounded response time, tenant isolation and useful behavior during regional failures. The team must place processing, data and asynchronous work.

Predict · 30 seconds

Does adding servers automatically guarantee a dependable exam service?

Show answer & explanation
No. Shared bottlenecks, state coordination, network failures and retry behavior can still lose or duplicate work. Specify the critical service, ownership of state and failure semantics, then test them.

Teaching scenario; not a report of a real incident.

MAP · Concept & visual04 / 20 · 3 min

Distribution adds opportunities and obligations

A networked interaction has failure states that a local call does not.
Pause & discuss

Can a client tell from a timeout whether the server processed its request?

Show answer & explanation
Usually not. The request may have failed before processing, or the server may have processed it and lost the reply. The protocol must handle that ambiguity.

Separate logical layers from physical tiers

Layers describe responsibilities; tiers describe deployment locations.
Pause & discuss

Does a four-layer design require four physical servers?

Show answer & explanation
No. Several layers can share a tier, or one layer can span multiple machines. Placement should follow quality and operational requirements.

Which observation establishes durable submission?

TRADE-OFF · Study an example10 / 20 · 4 min

Worked example: a scholarship application upload

Worked example · try, then reveal

Design a response that avoids duplicate applications.

Show answer & explanation
Use a stable application/submission identifier and server-side duplicate handling. Provide a status endpoint that reports the committed record. A repeated request for the same identifier should return the existing outcome or follow a documented update rule rather than silently creating another application.

Model reasoning for this practice case. Different choices can be defensible under different stated constraints.

Replication introduces coordination choices

During a partition, immediate consistency and continued access can conflict.
Pause & discuss

Can every isolated region promise immediate globally consistent acceptance?

Show answer & explanation
Not without the coordination needed to establish that guarantee. Specify whether a region rejects, queues provisionally or accepts under a weaker consistency model, and what the user is told.

TRADE-OFF · Evaluate the cost14 / 20 · 3 min

Quality of service needs end-to-end measures

Averages can hide peak-time and tail behavior.
Pause & discuss

Why is average response time alone weak evidence for exam submission?

Show answer & explanation
A small fraction of very slow or failed requests can be critical at a deadline. Examine the latency distribution, completion and durability behavior under representative peak load and failures.

EVIDENCE · Verify the claim15 / 20 · 2 min

Make the evidence inspectable

AI can propose placements and failure tests. Validate consistency, durable acknowledgment and retry assumptions; an architecture diagram alone does not establish these guarantees.
Discuss · evidence and AI

Which evidence item would most directly support or overturn your case decision?

Show answer & explanation
Use an artifact linked to the governing requirement and the current case boundary. Architecture: State ownership, tiers, trust and network boundaries; Load tests: Representative peak and tail-latency results; Failure tests: Partition, retries, durability and tenant isolation. Name what result would make you reopen the verdict. A document title without its relevant result is insufficient.

EVIDENCE · Collaborate & defend16 / 20 · 4 min

Stress the decision together

Collaborative activity · rotate roles

A regional partition occurs five minutes before the submission deadline. What must your architecture make explicit?

Show answer & explanation
Whether the region can issue durable acceptance, only provisional receipt or no acceptance; how deadlines are interpreted; how status and reconciliation work; and what the student sees. Verify the chosen behavior with fault injection.

Does using SaaS transfer all responsibility to the supplier?
