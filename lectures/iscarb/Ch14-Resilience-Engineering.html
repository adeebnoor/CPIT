CRISIS · Start with the case01 / 20 · 2 min

Resilience Engineering

Constructed teaching scenario · Chapter 14 · 2 minutes

A regional ambulance-dispatch platform depends on dispatch servers, hospital feeds, crew tablets and identity services. A partial infrastructure failure must not stop emergency calls from becoming an ambulance dispatch.

Predict · 30 seconds

Must every feature stay online for the system to be resilient?

Show answer & explanation
No. Identify and preserve the critical service at an agreed minimum level, potentially through degraded or manual operation. Plan safe recovery and reinstatement of the remaining functions.

Teaching scenario; not a report of a real incident.

MAP · Concept & visual04 / 20 · 3 min

Resilience assumes disruption will happen

Resilience complements prevention; it does not replace it.
Pause & discuss

Is a system with a tested manual fallback necessarily highly reliable?

Show answer & explanation
No. The fallback can improve resilience after disruption while normal service still fails frequently. The properties need different evidence.

MAP · Concept & visual07 / 20 · 3 min

Use resistance, recognition and recovery

Detection without a response and owner is not an operational resilience plan.
Pause & discuss

What is the difference between recovery and reinstatement?

Show answer & explanation
Recovery restores useful service, possibly in a degraded mode. Reinstatement returns to normal operation with consistency, controls and readiness verified.

MAP · Concept & visual08 / 20 · 3 min

Expose shared dependencies

A duplicate application does not remove a shared dependency.
Pause & discuss

What evidence would support the claim that failover is independent?

Show answer & explanation
A dependency map and failure exercises that include identity, network, power, data and operational access. Test the full critical-service path rather than only process restart.

People are part of the fallback

TRADE-OFF · Study an example10 / 20 · 4 min

Worked example: campus incident reporting

Worked example · try, then reveal

Specify a bounded degraded mode and reinstatement step.

Show answer & explanation
Use the approved duty phone and a numbered incident log while the portal is down. Name the responsible officer and escalation limit. On recovery, reconcile logged incidents before importing them and verify no report is lost or duplicated.

Model reasoning for this practice case. Different choices can be defensible under different stated constraints.

Make degraded operation explicit

A fallback needs entry criteria, an owner, operating limits and exit criteria.
Pause & discuss

What risk appears if a queued dispatch is replayed after manual handling?

Show answer & explanation
The same incident may be assigned twice. Reconcile identities and status before replay and define how conflicting records are resolved.

TRADE-OFF · Evaluate the cost14 / 20 · 3 min

Test continuity and restoration separately

Recovery-time and recovery-point targets describe different losses.
Pause & discuss

Does restoring servers prove that no incident records were lost?

Show answer & explanation
No. Restoration time is distinct from the allowable data-loss window. Verify data integrity, reconciliation and the recovery point in addition to time.

EVIDENCE · Verify the claim15 / 20 · 2 min

Make the evidence inspectable

AI can summarize incident timelines. Validate event ordering and omitted events; the duty team must own prioritization, emergency actions and reinstatement.
Discuss · evidence and AI

Which evidence item would most directly support or overturn your case decision?

Show answer & explanation
Use an artifact linked to the governing requirement and the current case boundary. Dependency map: Technical services and human roles; Exercise records: Observed continuity, recovery and data loss; Reinstatement checks: Reconciliation, consistency and safe exit. Name what result would make you reopen the verdict. A document title without its relevant result is insufficient.

EVIDENCE · Collaborate & defend16 / 20 · 4 min

Stress the decision together

Collaborative activity · rotate roles

The outage crosses a shift change. What must the fallback preserve?

Show answer & explanation
Ownership, pending incidents, decisions and escalation state must transfer clearly. Test handover and communication as part of the continuity claim, not just server failover.

Why repeat an exercise after changing the identity provider?
