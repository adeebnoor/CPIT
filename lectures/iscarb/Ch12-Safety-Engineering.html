Safety Engineering

Constructed teaching scenario · Chapter 12 · 2 minutes

A hospital infusion pump is ready for a weekend software release. Dose computation passes regression tests. After reconnecting, a sensor can briefly return a plausible but stale glucose value. The release decision must account for that condition.

Predict · 30 seconds

Do passed functional tests establish safety?

Show answer & explanation
No. They support the functions and conditions they tested. Safety needs a hazard-based argument covering stale inputs, timing, operators and the clinical environment, with specific controls and evidence.

Teaching scenario; not a report of a real incident.

Safety depends on context, not just the absence of software failures.
Pause & discuss

Can correctly calculated output be unsafe?

Show answer & explanation
Yes. The calculation may use stale or inappropriate input, or the correct output may violate a safety constraint in the current context.

Hazard is not the accident

Risk analysis considers likelihood and consequence; categories are not universally interchangeable numbers.
Pause & discuss

Classify “a stale glucose value is accepted as current.”

Show answer & explanation
It is a hazardous condition or a contributor to one. An incorrect dose and resulting injury are subsequent consequences; the full causal path must be analyzed.

MAP · Concept & visual08 / 20 · 3 min

Reduce risk in a deliberate order

Safety requirements often prohibit hazardous actions and specify the response to abnormal states.
Pause & discuss

Write a requirement for stale input after reconnect.

Show answer & explanation
For example: automatic dose delivery shall be inhibited until sensor freshness and validity satisfy the approved clinical criteria; the system shall signal the condition and follow the approved fallback. Thresholds must come from authorized clinical requirements.

TRADE-OFF · Study an example10 / 20 · 4 min

Worked example: an automatic laboratory door

Worked example · try, then reveal

Connect the hazard to a verifiable safety claim.

Show answer & explanation
Claim: closure is prevented while occupancy is detected within the validated operating conditions. Requirement: inhibit closure and signal an interlock fault under the specified fault conditions. Evidence: occupancy, boundary, sensor-failure and recovery tests. The case must address false negatives and the assumed sensor coverage.

Model reasoning for this practice case. Different choices can be defensible under different stated constraints.

TRADE-OFF · Evaluate the cost14 / 20 · 3 min

Acceptable risk needs accountable justification

A classroom risk matrix is a reasoning aid, not permission to accept patient risk.
Pause & discuss

Is “low probability” sufficient to accept a potentially catastrophic hazard?

Show answer & explanation
No. Consequence, uncertainty, available controls and applicable obligations matter. The authorized safety process determines acceptability; a low point estimate does not settle it.

EVIDENCE · Verify the claim15 / 20 · 2 min

Make the evidence inspectable

AI can draft hazard candidates. It cannot establish completeness of hazard analysis or accept clinical risk; specialists must review evidence and sign off.
Discuss · evidence and AI

Which evidence item would most directly support or overturn your case decision?

Show answer & explanation
Use an artifact linked to the governing requirement and the current case boundary. Hazard trace: Hazard → control → safety requirement; Verification: Adverse-condition tests and independent reviews; Safety case: Bounded claim, argument, evidence and owner. Name what result would make you reopen the verdict. A document title without its relevant result is insufficient.

EVIDENCE · Collaborate & defend16 / 20 · 4 min

Stress the decision together

Collaborative activity · rotate roles

Freshness validation adds a delay and clinicians request a bypass. How should the team respond?

Show answer & explanation
Analyze the clinical and software risks of both delay and bypass with authorized domain specialists. Do not silently remove the safeguard. Any approved alternative needs explicit constraints, traceable requirements and supporting verification.

Retain, revise or replace only when the reasoning warrants it. A changed fact does not mechanically require a changed verdict.

Readiness 1 · course-written practice

How does a hazard differ from an accident?

Show answer & explanation
A hazard is a condition with potential for harm; an accident is the harmful event.
Readiness 2 · course-written practice

Why is reliability insufficient for safety?

Show answer & explanation
Reliable implementation can faithfully execute an unsafe requirement or operate on invalid assumptions.
Readiness 3 · course-written practice

What belongs in a safety case?

Show answer & explanation
A bounded safety claim, a reasoned argument, supporting evidence and explicit assumptions.
Readiness 4 · course-written practice

When should safety analysis be revisited?

Show answer & explanation
When changes, incidents or new evidence affect hazards, controls or the assumptions of the safety argument.
