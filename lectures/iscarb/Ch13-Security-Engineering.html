CRISIS · Start with the case01 / 20 · 2 min

Security Engineering

Constructed teaching scenario · Chapter 13 · 2 minutes

A university research-data portal opens to external collaborators next week. It uses SSO, MFA, roles and audit logging. Unpublished datasets are the asset. The team needs evidence that those controls enforce the intended access policy.

Predict · 30 seconds

Does “MFA enabled” establish that every dataset is protected?

Show answer & explanation
No. MFA strengthens authentication. Dataset authorization, session handling, service accounts, export paths and recovery still require requirements and tests.

Teaching scenario; not a report of a real incident.

MAP · Concept & visual04 / 20 · 3 min

Start with assets and losses

Prioritize protection according to the loss and the exposure, not the popularity of a tool.
Pause & discuss

Is a publicly readable export endpoint an asset or a vulnerability?

Show answer & explanation
If policy requires restricted access, the unrestricted endpoint is a vulnerability. The research data is the asset; unauthorized disclosure is the loss.

MAP · Concept & visual05 / 20 · 3 min

Security protects several kinds of value

Security failures can become reliability, safety or availability failures.
Pause & discuss

Which property is directly threatened by altered research results?

Show answer & explanation
Integrity is directly threatened because the results have been changed improperly. Depending on downstream use, the change may also harm safety, trust or availability.

MAP · Concept & visual07 / 20 · 3 min

An organization must define its policy

A technical control cannot resolve an unspecified authorization policy.
Pause & discuss

Who should decide whether an external collaborator may export a dataset?

Show answer & explanation
The authorized data owner and institutional policy establish permission. The engineering team implements and verifies that decision rather than inventing it in code.

MAP · Concept & visual08 / 20 · 3 min

Risk analysis becomes security requirements

Security requirements include prevention, detection, recovery and accountability.
Pause & discuss

Write a requirement that is stronger than “the portal shall be secure.”

Show answer & explanation
For example: an external collaborator shall access a dataset only when an active, dataset-specific permission exists; revoked permission shall block subsequent requests under the defined revocation policy. Verify both allowed and denied requests.

TRADE-OFF · Concept & visual09 / 20 · 3 min

Authentication is not authorization

Check permissions on the server at every protected operation.
Pause & discuss

Can hiding the export button enforce authorization?

Show answer & explanation
No. A client can call the endpoint directly. Enforce the access policy server-side and test requests that bypass the user interface.

TRADE-OFF · Study an example10 / 20 · 4 min

Worked example: a confidential committee workspace

Worked example · try, then reveal

Specify the missing control and verification.

Show answer & explanation
Enforce committee membership on the server for each document and export request. Test a member, a non-member and a revoked member against direct endpoints. Retain access-decision logs. Hiding links supports usability but is not the access control.

Model reasoning for this practice case. Different choices can be defensible under different stated constraints.

TRADE-OFF · Deepen the mechanism12 / 20 · 3 min

Use defense in depth deliberately

Layers should address distinct failure paths; more layers can also add complexity.
Pause & discuss

Why are logs alone insufficient to protect a dataset?

Show answer & explanation
They can support detection and investigation but do not necessarily block exfiltration. Define who monitors them, what triggers response and how access is contained.

Challenge the attractive answer

Poll 2 · analyze

A portal passes one penetration test. Which conclusion is justified?

Show answer & explanation

TRADE-OFF · Evaluate the cost14 / 20 · 3 min

Controls create trade-offs

Assess security together with usability, operations and recovery.
Pause & discuss

Should every failed login permanently lock an account?

Show answer & explanation
That can create a denial-of-service route. Use a policy appropriate to the threat model, with tested rate limits, recovery and monitoring rather than an unqualified permanent lock.

EVIDENCE · Verify the claim15 / 20 · 2 min

Make the evidence inspectable

AI may suggest abuse cases or review checklists. Validate each against the real architecture and approved testing scope; do not upload confidential datasets to unapproved tools.
Discuss · evidence and AI

Which evidence item would most directly support or overturn your case decision?

Show answer & explanation
Use an artifact linked to the governing requirement and the current case boundary. Threat trace: Asset → threat → requirement → control; Negative tests: Unauthorized and revoked access attempts; Residual risk: Open findings, response plan and accepting owner. Name what result would make you reopen the verdict. A document title without its relevant result is insufficient.

EVIDENCE · Collaborate & defend16 / 20 · 4 min

Stress the decision together

Collaborative activity · rotate roles

A collaborator’s account is compromised after a successful MFA login. Which assumptions fail?

Show answer & explanation
The authenticated session can no longer be treated as a sufficient indication of legitimate intent. Assess least privilege, session revocation, unusual export detection and containment; revise the acceptance argument using those paths.

Retain, revise or replace only when the reasoning warrants it. A changed fact does not mechanically require a changed verdict.

EVIDENCE · Bound the assurance17 / 20 · 3 min

Test against misuse, not only intended use

Passing penetration tests is evidence about a tested scope, not proof of no vulnerabilities.
Pause & discuss

What would test dataset isolation between collaborators?

Show answer & explanation
Use authorized test accounts with distinct dataset permissions. Attempt direct and indirect reads, exports and modifications across those permissions and inspect denial behavior and audit records.

Readiness 1 · course-written practice

Distinguish a threat from a vulnerability.

Show answer & explanation
A threat is a potential source of loss; a vulnerability is a weakness that can be exploited.
Readiness 2 · course-written practice

Why are authentication and authorization separate?

Show answer & explanation
One establishes identity; the other decides permitted actions on resources.
Readiness 3 · course-written practice

What makes a security requirement testable?

Show answer & explanation
An asset, actor, allowed or prohibited behavior, conditions and a verification method.
Readiness 4 · course-written practice

Why does security assurance remain conditional?

Show answer & explanation
Threats, configuration, dependencies and attack methods change; tests only cover their defined scope.
