Dependable Systems

Constructed teaching scenario · Chapter 10 · 2 minutes

At 22:14, a coastal desalination plant receives a control upgrade. Pump P-204 reports intermittent high pressure. The backup train switches automatically. At handover, an operator silences a repeating alarm. The assurance file covers normal operation.

Predict · 30 seconds

What evidence would you demand before continuing for another hour?

Show answer & explanation
A defensible starting point is time-aligned pressure, alarm and failover logs for the deployed firmware, plus the approved safe-operating limits. Hold or constrain operation if those limits cannot be verified. The scenario alone does not prove the root cause or justify unrestricted continuation.

Teaching scenario; not a report of a real incident.

Dependability is a bundle of promises

One event can threaten several properties; trust needs evidence for each relevant promise.
Pause & discuss

A plant is online but delivers unsafe water. Which promise is broken?

Show answer & explanation
Safety is directly threatened. Being online does not establish useful availability or correct service; reliability may also be compromised. Property names organize the analysis; they do not make the properties independent.

MAP · Concept & visual05 / 20 · 3 min

Separate fault, error and failure

A fault can remain dormant. An error can be detected before it becomes a service failure.
Pause & discuss

Does every software fault produce a failure on every run?

Show answer & explanation
No. The fault must be activated under particular conditions, propagate into an erroneous state, and escape containment before an externally observable failure occurs.

Poll 1 · retrieve

A service has short, frequent failures but recovers quickly. What can be true?

Show answer & explanation
A. High availability with weak reliability — High availability can coexist with frequent interruptions when repair is fast. Reliability concerns failure-free service over a stated interval; availability also depends on restoration.

MAP · Concept & visual07 / 20 · 3 min

Follow the failure across the system

Investigate interactions across layers before blaming the last person who touched the system.
Pause & discuss

Is the silenced alarm enough to classify this as operator error?

Show answer & explanation
No. Inspect alarm design, workload, training, procedures and time-stamped events. Hardware, software and operational causes can interact; silencing the alarm is an observation, not a complete causal explanation.

MAP · Concept & visual08 / 20 · 3 min

Redundancy adds capacity; diversity changes failure risk

Independence is an assumption to investigate, not a benefit obtained merely by duplication.
Pause & discuss

Would two identical controllers remove a shared software defect?

Show answer & explanation
No. The same input may activate the same defect in both. Diverse implementations may reduce some correlated failures, but shared requirements, inputs and infrastructure can still fail together.

Three complementary routes to dependability

Prevention, detection and tolerance complement one another.
Pause & discuss

Which route lets a service continue after a component fails?

Show answer & explanation
Fault tolerance, provided the fault is detected and contained and the recovery mechanism works. It does not remove the need for fault avoidance or defect detection.

TRADE-OFF · Study an example10 / 20 · 4 min

Worked example: a campus access gate

Worked example · try, then reveal

Build a bounded decision using FIT, BOUND, ACT and EVIDENCE.

Show answer & explanation
FIT: distinguish access availability from life safety. BOUND: use the fallback only while emergency egress and the approved access rules remain effective. ACT: enable the approved supervised fallback and assign an operator. EVIDENCE: inspect outage logs and test egress and authorization behavior. This is one defensible example; local safety and security requirements determine the actual fallback.

Model reasoning for this practice case. Different choices can be defensible under different stated constraints.

A dependable process leaves a trace

Iteration can coexist with traceability, change control and independent checks.
Pause & discuss

Can agile development be used for a dependable system?

Show answer & explanation
Yes, with suitable controls. Iterative development and automated testing remain useful, but critical requirements, evidence, change impact and release approval must stay explicit. Informal iteration alone is not an assurance argument.

Two backup trains share one power supply. What is the strongest concern?

TRADE-OFF · Evaluate the cost14 / 20 · 3 min

Why “more dependable” is not a complete decision

The next increment must be justified against consequences, obligations and new failure modes.
Pause & discuss

Should we always add another redundant component?

Show answer & explanation
No. Assess the risk reduction, cost, common dependencies and added complexity. A duplicate can introduce coordination failures or maintenance burdens. Some safety obligations remain mandatory even when expensive.

Make the evidence inspectable

AI may organize logs or suggest failure hypotheses. Verify them against the actual baseline; a named engineer owns the release decision.

Which evidence item would most directly support or overturn your case decision?

EVIDENCE · Collaborate & defend16 / 20 · 4 min

Stress the decision together

Collaborative activity · rotate roles

The available commissioning evidence covers the previous firmware. Does your verdict survive?

Show answer & explanation
Confidence in the deployed version is no longer established by those results alone. Assess change impact and require targeted verification for the current baseline. Constrain or hold operation where the unmet evidence concerns a critical function.

Retain, revise or replace only when the reasoning warrants it. A changed fact does not mechanically require a changed verdict.

EVIDENCE · Bound the assurance17 / 20 · 3 min

A proof is only as broad as its model

Formal specification and verification strengthen assurance; they do not prove the entire real world safe.
Pause & discuss

A controller is formally verified. Can we skip commissioning?

Show answer & explanation
No. Commissioning checks the deployed configuration and its environment. A proof can establish a property of a model while the real installation violates the model’s assumptions.

Readiness 1 · course-written practice

Name the five dependability properties.

Show answer & explanation
Availability, reliability, safety, security and resilience.
Readiness 2 · course-written practice

Distinguish a fault from a failure.

Show answer & explanation
A fault is a defect; a failure is an externally observable violation of expected service. An erroneous state can connect the two.
Readiness 3 · course-written practice

Why can duplicate controllers fail together?

Show answer & explanation
Shared specifications, code, inputs, power or environment can create correlated failure.
Readiness 4 · course-written practice

What remains outside a formal proof?

Show answer & explanation
Anything beyond the proved model and its assumptions, including unmodeled environmental or operational conditions.
