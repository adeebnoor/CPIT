CRISIS · Start with the case01 / 20 · 2 min

Software Reuse

Constructed teaching scenario · Chapter 15 · 2 minutes

A government permitting platform needs document signing in eight weeks. It can build a module, adapt a framework or reuse a commercial service. Delivery speed matters, but requirements fit, support, licensing and integration are uncertain.

Predict · 30 seconds

Is the option with the least new code necessarily the best choice?

Show answer & explanation
No. Compare requirements fit, criticality, adaptation and integration effort, total lifecycle cost, supplier support and control over change. Reuse shifts work and risk rather than eliminating them.

Teaching scenario; not a report of a real incident.

Is adapting a commercial application the same as copying a helper function?

Show answer & explanation
Both are reuse, but they operate at different scales and create different configuration, integration, control and lifecycle obligations.

Benefits and costs arrive together

Reduced development effort is not the same as reduced total cost.
Pause & discuss

Why can a free reusable library still be expensive?

MAP · Concept & visual07 / 20 · 3 min

A framework calls your code

Inversion of control distinguishes a framework from a library that your code simply calls.
Pause & discuss

Who controls the main lifecycle in a typical application framework?

Show answer & explanation
The framework controls the main lifecycle. Application-specific code is registered at extension points and invoked by the framework when the relevant event or lifecycle stage occurs.

MAP · Concept & visual08 / 20 · 3 min

A product line manages commonality and variation

A software product line deliberately manages a family of related products.
Pause & discuss

Is maintaining unrelated copies of an application automatically a product line?

Show answer & explanation
No. A product line requires managed common assets and explicit variability. Uncontrolled forks can lose the reuse and maintenance benefits.

TRADE-OFF · Study an example10 / 20 · 4 min

Worked example: a campus room-booking service

Worked example · try, then reveal

Show how a bounded reuse decision could be justified.

Show answer & explanation
Reuse is defensible if the existing application enforces required booking permissions, conflicts and auditability through supported configuration. Test representative workflows and migration. Build or adapt differently if an essential policy cannot be met without an unsupportable fork.

Model reasoning for this practice case. Different choices can be defensible under different stated constraints.

The reusable system meets today’s needs but loses support next year. What changes?

Show answer & explanation

Expose the integration gap

A feature checklist is weaker than evidence from representative integration tests.
Pause & discuss

If a signing service supports signatures but not your audit records, is fit established?

Show answer & explanation
No. Auditability is part of the required service. Determine whether supported integration can provide it and verify the resulting evidence path.

EVIDENCE · Verify the claim15 / 20 · 2 min

Make the evidence inspectable

AI may compare candidate documentation. Check versions, licenses and claimed capabilities against authoritative product records and actual integration tests.
Discuss · evidence and AI

Which evidence item would most directly support or overturn your case decision?

Show answer & explanation
Use an artifact linked to the governing requirement and the current case boundary. Fit-gap matrix: Essential requirements and verified behavior; Integration prototype: Representative workflow and quality tests; Lifecycle record: Support, licenses, dependencies and exit path. Name what result would make you reopen the verdict. A document title without its relevant result is insufficient.

EVIDENCE · Collaborate & defend16 / 20 · 4 min

Stress the decision together

Collaborative activity · rotate roles

A supplier’s new version removes a required interface. How should the team respond?

Show answer & explanation
Compare supported adaptation, bounded version retention and replacement, including security and support consequences. Reopen the original fit and lifecycle assumptions and document the chosen migration or constraint.

Retain, revise or replace only when the reasoning warrants it. A changed fact does not mechanically require a changed verdict.

What would make a previously suitable reused service unfit?
