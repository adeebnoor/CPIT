RESEARCH · TEACHING · PROVENANCE

From one course to an evolving design system.

CPIT-455 became a three-cycle design program: CIMT connected curriculum to practice, IMAM added culturally and ethically situated learning, and HIMMA introduced a template-governed GenAI workflow for scale. This archive keeps public teaching artifacts beside the research story without exposing participant-identifiable material.
