CORE IDEA

Iteration can be responsive without being cumulative.

Fidelity debt is the gap between design requirements accumulated across cycles and the subset visibly realized in the current artifact. A later design may improve technically while inherited purposes, contexts, learning structures, or accountability commitments become implicit, untraceable, or absent.

Three design principles derived from the case

01Specify inheritance, not topics.

Carry prior requirements forward as acceptance criteria.

02Make every disposition explicit.

Preserve, revise, or retire with rationale; silence is not a design decision.

03Govern release with people.

Generated artifacts pass a human fidelity gate before release.
