DESIGN PROVENANCE

Framework lineage

This archive documents an evolving instructional design program. Framework labels changed across course versions; the archived pages are preserved as historical instructional artifacts.

Cycle 1 · Spring 2025

CIMT

Curriculum–industry alignment provided the initial design engine and established the first cycle of the program.

Cycle 2 · Fall 2025

AMIR / EMAM–IMAM lineage

The Fall 2025 archive contains AMIR and CIMT labels, while individual instructional artifacts also preserve EMAM/IMAM terminology from the evolving culturally calibrated design.

Cycle 3 · Spring 2026

HIMMA

A template-governed lecture design cycle extending the earlier work into a more explicit instructional architecture.

Archive note. This site is a public record of instructional materials, not a claim that every historical framework label is terminologically identical. Individual archived pages may retain the label used when that version was produced.

COURSE CONTENT

Learning modules

Eight topics spanning dependability, security, reuse, distributed systems, and large-scale systems engineering.

Chapter 11

Reliability Engineering

Fault tolerance, N-version programming, and reliability-oriented design.
Chapter 12

Safety Engineering

Hazard analysis, safety cases, and engineering for critical systems.
Chapter 13

Security Engineering

CIA triad, STRIDE, security reasoning, and culturally calibrated applications.

Resilience Engineering

The 4Rs and sociotechnical approaches to resilient systems.

Software Reuse

Application frameworks, product lines, and systematic software reuse.

Components, interfaces, composition, and reusable system architecture.

Systems of Systems

Complexity, governance, emergence, and engineering mega-systems.
