ISCARB LEARNING SYSTEM

See it. Decide it. Defend it.

The course is organized around learner action rather than slide density. Native source visuals carry technical detail; the lecture surface keeps only the reasoning needed to move from evidence to a defensible decision.

SOURCE

Use the original figure or diagram when it carries engineering information. The source stays authoritative; decoration never replaces evidence.

Assessment asks for inspectable evidence, a decision boundary, and named human sign-off. AI may prepare; it does not own assurance.
