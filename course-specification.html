1 · iSCARB and the course specification إطار iSCARB وتوصيف المقرر

iSCARB (Interactive System for Case-based Analysis, Reasoning & Boundaries) is the teaching and assessment framework of this course. Class time teaches the engineering mechanism; a separate, bounded assignment asks each student to own, bound and defend a decision with inspectable evidence, and the final sign-off remains human. The table shows which section of the specification each element serves. See also the iSCARB methodology and the Saudi alignment page.
