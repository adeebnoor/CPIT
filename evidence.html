FOR INSTRUCTORS · EVIDENCE OF LEARNING

Is the course working?

Three measures, computed in your browser from files you already have. Nothing is uploaded or stored.

  1. Concept gain. The 14-item concept inventory (kept privately with the instructor) taken in week 1 of Chapter 12 and again after Chapter 20, both as Blackboard tests. Reports the normalized gain ⟨g⟩ = (post − pre) / (100 − pre).
  2. Peer-instruction gain. The class votes in each lecture. On the episode close, presenter mode offers Export class evidence (CSV). Reports % correct before and after discussion, per act.
  3. Ownership. The micro-viva record sheet, downloaded as CSV and completed. Reports how often the oral explanation agrees with the written work.

Reference points (not targets): traditional lecture courses commonly report ⟨g⟩ around 0.2 and interactive-engagement courses 0.35–0.5 (Hake, 1998). In peer instruction, % correct usually rises after discussion when the first-round result is between about 35% and 70% (Crouch & Mazur, 2001). Small sections give wide uncertainty; read the N.

1 · Concept inventory: pre/post gain

Download each test's column from the Blackboard Grade Center (Work offline → Download, selected column, comma-delimited). Choose the two files. Students are matched by the identifier column.

2 · Peer instruction: votes before and after discussion

Choose one or more class-evidence CSV files exported from the lectures (several sections or chapters at once is fine).

3 · Micro-viva: does the explanation agree with the written work?

Choose the completed record sheets (CSV from the micro-viva page). The agreement column should say agrees, partly or does not agree.
