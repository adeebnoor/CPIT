THE CORE IDEA

Fact-Based Reasoning (FBR)

FITWhat fits now, and why?
BOUNDWhere does fit stop?
ACTWhat professional action follows?
EVIDENCEWhat can another person inspect?
REFITRetain, revise or replace.
EVALUATION

Weekly FBR rubric · 4 points

Criterion1 point · defensibleWeak / partial
FITSelects the relevant mechanism and explains why it fits this specific use.Generic definition or weak context link.
BOUNDStates an observable boundary and trigger for reopening the decision.Vague boundary with no inspectable trigger.
ACT + EVIDENCEProduces a concrete action/artifact and evidence another professional can inspect.Intent without actionable evidence or ownership.
REFITExplains whether new evidence crossed the boundary and justifies retain/revise/replace.Changes answer without tracing the boundary or evidence.
GENAI & ACADEMIC INTEGRITY

AI may assist. It may not own the judgment.

Permitted support

  • Language editing and clarity.
  • Brainstorming alternatives or counter-evidence.
  • Drafting tests, summaries, probes or candidate code.
  • Structuring evidence for review.

Human responsibility

  • You own every submitted decision.
  • You must be able to explain every sentence.
  • AI may not own certification, approval, risk acceptance or final assurance.
  • Unclear counter-evidence must be independently verified.

ORAL VERIFICATION

What happens after submission?

Micro-viva

A short oral check verifies ownership. You should be able to explain your claim, evidence, boundary, counter-evidence and verdict.

Feed-forward

Use comments and oral verification to improve the next bounded decision, not only the current score.
