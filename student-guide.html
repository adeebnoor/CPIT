STUDENT & INSTRUCTOR GUIDE

One familiar rhythm.
A new engineering challenge.

The original concepts stay visible. iSCARB supplies the reasoning practice around them.

Navigate the visual lectures

  1. Start with the chapter map. Connect the concepts and check your role, then move to the classroom case.
  2. Follow the visual overview. Each concept has a purposeful diagram, source figure or technical comparison and a short takeaway.
  3. Open Full explanation for detail. The arrow controls move between the 20 classroom slides. Select Slides or press O to open the slide index. Use Chapter map to return to the overview.
  4. Attempt before revealing. Read WHAT IT MEANS, attempt the prompt, then open Model answer. At the three named stations, think, discuss and update the same practice card.

Arrow keys navigate; Home and End move to the first and final slide. O opens the slide index, C the Decision card and N the instructor notes. Escape closes a dialog; Tab moves between controls. Local saving remains separate from Blackboard submission.

Required study + AI-only assignment: exact scope

  1. Review the named source selections and the chapter AI segment before the assignment.
  2. Read the worked example and rubric first. The example teaches quality; it is not the assessed case.
  3. Apply one assigned source concept to the AI-containing system, then make a bounded engineering decision.
  4. Commit Part A before STRESS, revise the decision, complete human review, and download the Blackboard JSON.

Assessment scope: the five chapter objectives, classroom concepts and named required self-study. The full source ledger supports clarification. Optional toolkit activities are enrichment unless specifically assigned. Additional required source topics must be announced in Blackboard before assessment.

Workload and feedback

Initial planning budget: approximately 35–55 active minutes total, including required preparation and the AI-only assignment. Report actual time; this planning target does not affect the grade.

Keep answers compact and technical. A table, calculation or structured artifact may replace prose. The grader rewards mechanism, boundary, evidence and accountability—not length or fluency.

Submit once through Blackboard: upload the JSON produced by the assignment page. Blackboard is the official identity and receipt record. PDF/Markdown are optional personal copies unless the instructor requests them.

Already started? Use the previous-edition link inside the current assignment to resume or export old work with the same ID and browser. Do not redo a completed submission unless the instructor explicitly requests it. Grades and deadlines remain those announced in Blackboard.

For students

  1. Open the chapter route. Read the five objectives and the fictional case. The classroom sequence includes the original foundations and three short interactions.
  2. Try before revealing. Complete the three stations in your own words. Open the authored hint or illustrative discussion afterward.
  3. Use Required self-study. A named topic studied after class remains required. The source coverage map links the topics to the classroom and original source slides.
  4. Complete the AI-only assignment. Preserve the initial judgment, reveal STRESS, then justify RETAIN, REVISE or REPLACE.
  5. Review and submit. Declare AI assistance, download Blackboard JSON and upload that one file to the Blackboard assignment.

For instructors

Begin with the chapter goal and case, then teach the source concepts in order. Use only three planned interaction stops: Station 1, Station 2 and Station 3. Per-slide questions can serve self-study without becoming another in-class task.

For chapters allocated two weeks in the supplied syllabus, use two blocks and pause after Station 2. For one-week chapters, plan 75–90 minutes of explanation and the named reading and preparation check, using the total-workload targets above. These are estimates: preserve your actual timetable.

If a required concept is unfinished, schedule it for the next block or explicitly assign its source review. Do not treat the toolkit as a second compulsory lecture.

Use N for the current slide’s teaching notes. Use the source coverage map to record what remains to be taught in your LMS. The website records material availability, not attendance or teaching completion.

What stays consistent

CRISIS → MAP → TRADE-OFF → EVIDENCE → VERDICT remains the reasoning thread. Every after-class task is now an AI-containing system and keeps the familiar FIT → AI ARTIFACT → BOUND → ACT + EVIDENCE → STRESS → REFIT sequence.

Readiness checks support reflection. Filled fields, reviewed pages and practice scores do not certify that an explanation is correct. The instructor judges the reasoning.

The updated classroom rhythm

Follow 20 main slides. Read WHAT IT MEANS as short points, use Full explanation for detail, and attempt before opening Model answer. At each of three stations: think, discuss with a partner, then update the same card. The timer and authored hint support practice; they do not grade you.

Station 1: claim + evidence. Station 2: mechanism + warrant + uncertainty. Station 3: changed constraint + counter-evidence + revised decision and owner. The separate assignment applies the same method to a new case. Export a backup of your practice card; do not copy it as the answer to the assessed case.

Use Required self-study for exact source selections and all five practice questions. Source originals load on demand. For offline use, get the ZIP rather than saving only the HTML shell. Earlier classroom and assignment drafts remain available through their recovery links.

Move between slides without searching for the arrows

Enter: next slide. Backspace: previous slide. In desktop presentation mode, a single mouse click on non-interactive slide content advances; Shift + click goes back. The existing arrow keys and arrow buttons remain available.

Buttons, links, enlarged figures, input fields and dialogs keep their normal behavior. Enter and Backspace remain editing keys while you write; Enter activates a focused button or link. Mouse slide navigation pauses in classroom stations, Reading view and narrow/mobile layouts, so interaction and text selection do not skip slides. Keyboard navigation still works outside controls in Reading view.

The same controls are included in new offline ZIP downloads. Previously downloaded packages do not update themselves: download the current ZIP and export a backup of any existing card before switching copies.
