For students

  1. Read the situation.Look at the question and source diagram before choosing an answer.
  2. Explain your reasoning.Discuss with a partner or make a short note. Identify the property, supporting evidence and the limit of your decision.
  3. Check and revise.Open the model answer or reflection guidance, compare it with your reasoning, then close it to try again.
  4. Revisit what needs work.Use the Units menu to return to a concept. Enlarge the text and diagrams as needed.

For instructors

Open the lecture directly for the full presentation area. Start with the situation, pause for a prediction, discuss the diagram, then reveal the explanation.

  • Units: jump to a topic without stepping through every screen.
  • Notes: open teaching prompts when you need them. Notes are visible on the displayed screen, so close them before projecting if you want to keep the prompts private.
  • Text size: use A− / A+ to suit the room or device.
  • Screen (full screen): use the lecture control; press Escape to leave.
  • Help: shows the available keyboard shortcuts.

Common questions

Where are the other lectures and assignments?

They have been withdrawn for rebuilding. Chapter 10 is the only published lecture, and no assignments are currently published. Check the LMS for official announcements.

Does saving a response submit it?

No. Saving or exporting practice work does not submit anything to the instructor. Only the official LMS records assessed submissions.

Will my work appear on another device?

Practice responses and review marks stay in the browser where you created them. Download your practice work before changing devices or clearing browser data.

Can I study offline?

Download the Chapter 10 HTML file, then open it in a browser. Its diagrams and lesson controls are included. Links back to the course website require an internet connection.

How should I use AI?

Use it to explore explanations or alternatives, then verify its claims against the source. You should be able to explain your own evidence and decision. Follow the instructor’s LMS policy for assessed work.
