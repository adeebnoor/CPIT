COURSE SPECIFICATION

Official course profile

CourseCPIT-455 · Software Engineering (II)
PrerequisiteCPIT-251
Primary textIan Sommerville · Software Engineering · 10th ed.

Catalog focus

Advanced software-engineering principles and techniques spanning specification, design, testing, quality, processes and software-system engineering.

Why Ch. 10–17, then 20?

The official coverage explicitly includes Dependable Systems, Reliability, Safety, Security, Resilience, Software Reuse, Component-Based Software Engineering, Distributed Software Engineering and Systems of Systems.

Course Learning Outcomes

CLO 1Design dependability using redundancy and diversity.
CLO 2Write functional and non-functional dependability and security requirements.
CLO 3Apply reliability metrics to measurable reliability requirements.
CLO 4Design safety in safety-critical systems.
CLO 5Design and test secure systems.
CLO 6Design systems to support resilience.
CLO 7Identify elements involved in reuse and standardized reusable components.
CLO 8Describe reuse through composition of standardized reusable components.
CLO 9Explain distributed-systems engineering and architectures.
CLO 10Explain systems of systems and their engineering challenges.
CLO 11Design and implement test cases across testing strategies.
