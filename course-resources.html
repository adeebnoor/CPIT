Software Engineering II

Instructor: Professor Adeeb Noor
Institution: King Abdulaziz University
Prerequisite: CPIT-251
Primary text: Ian Sommerville, Software Engineering, 10th edition.

The course develops advanced software engineering knowledge in dependability, requirements, architecture, testing and software systems.

Current reading

Chapter 10 · Dependable systems. Use an authorized copy of the textbook alongside the seven source diagrams included in the lecture.

Focus on dependability properties, sociotechnical systems, redundancy, diversity, dependable processes and formal methods.
