Assignments and assessment

Assignment 1 uses the existing 4-point decision rubric. Assignment 2 retains its 5-point measurement rubric. Assignments 3–9 use five criteria and four performance levels (1, 0.75, 0.5, 0 per criterion), with a chapter-specific artifact. These task points are not course-grade percentages.

The core workflow remains stable while the artifact progresses: measure, trace, test, recover, compare, specify a contract, reason about remote failure, and integrate across owners. Each assessed case is fictional; any additional evidence must be marked proposed or unknown.
