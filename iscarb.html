CPIT-455 uses split delivery: disciplinary knowledge is taught In-Class, then Fact-Based Reasoning (FBR) is practised After-Class through a bounded professional decision. Every assessed judgment remains human-owned and must be defensible with inspectable evidence.

COURSE CONTEXT

Why chapters 10–17, then 20?

The sequence is intentional, not a missing-content error. It follows the advanced Software Engineering II spine used in the official CPIT-455 specification and current kickoff roadmap.

Advanced engineering spine

The selected chapters move from dependability and assurance into reuse, components, distribution, and systems-of-systems. Foundational software-engineering concepts are prerequisites rather than repeated here.
