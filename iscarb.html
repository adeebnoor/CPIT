Teach the discipline. Practise the judgment.

CPIT-455 uses split delivery: disciplinary knowledge is taught In-Class, then Fact-Based Reasoning (FBR) is practised After-Class through a bounded professional decision. Every assessed judgment remains human-owned and must be defensible with inspectable evidence.

COURSE CONTEXT

Why chapters 10–17, then 20?

The sequence is intentional: assurance first, then reuse/components/distribution, then systems-of-systems. Foundational chapters are prerequisite knowledge.

Advanced engineering spine

The selected chapters follow the CPIT-455 topic coverage and Sommerville's advanced dependability and software-engineering sequence.
