NEW · AI STUDY COACH

Study with ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, the iSCARB way.

Each chapter now has a file that AI assistants can read. The assistant explains from the approved lecture, asks you for your FIT, BOUND, ACT and EVIDENCE, and challenges vague claims. It does not write your assignment. Part A, STRESS and submission stay on this site.

Try it: paste this into your assistant and change the chapter number:

Read https://adeebnoor.github.io/CPIT/ai/iscarb-coach.md and https://adeebnoor.github.io/CPIT/ai/ch13.md, then coach me on Chapter 13.

Declare any AI use in the assignment’s AI declaration. Do not share your name or student ID with the assistant. If your assistant cannot open links, download the file and attach it.

RESEARCH BASIS

The framework behind defensible engineering judgment.

The FIT → BOUND → ACT → EVIDENCE → commit → STRESS → REFIT architecture is grounded in the iSCARB preprint. AI is used as a transfer/practice layer inside the software-engineering chapters: every chapter has a GenAI segment and an AI practice challenge, and the assessed assignment stays the chapter task rather than a separate AI-only outcome. The formal objectives, readings, self-checks and assessed assignments remain the chapter assessment contract described in the paper.
