RESEARCH BASIS

The framework behind defensible engineering judgment.

The FIT → BOUND → ACT → EVIDENCE → commit → STRESS → REFIT architecture is grounded in the iSCARB preprint. AI is used as a transfer/practice layer inside the software-engineering chapters; the formal objectives, readings, self-checks and assessed assignments remain the chapter assessment contract described in the paper.
