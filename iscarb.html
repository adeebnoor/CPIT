Focus for this chapter

  • Distinguish reliability from availability and define failure precisely.
  • Select and interpret POFOD, ROCOF/MTTF and AVAIL.
  • Write measurable functional and non-functional reliability requirements.
  • Connect fault tolerance and dependable programming to inspectable evidence.
  • Judge reliability tests against the operational profile they represent.

Assignment 2: one step harder — adds MEASURE to the same decision pattern. 5 points.
